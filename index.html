Executive
Summary

Malaysia’s communications and multimedia industry remained resilient in 2025, supported by sustained demand for connectivity, continued infrastructure investment and the growing role of digital services in everyday life.

Listed communications and multimedia companies recorded RM110.63 billion in market capitalisation, while industry revenue grew by 1.5% to RM49.65 billion. National connectivity also advanced, with 9.81 million premises passed with fibre, 95.7% 4G LTE coverage in populated areas and 29.0 million 5G subscriptions.

Across the wider ecosystem, digital inclusion, online safety, trusted transactions, content innovation and the continued growth of e-commerce and parcel delivery reinforced the industry’s role in Malaysia’s digital future.
